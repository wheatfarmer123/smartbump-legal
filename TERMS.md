## 2. Description of Service
SmartBump is a Discord bot that runs a shared advertising network. Servers that join the network choose a channel where advertisements from other servers are posted. In return, they can use `/bump` to post their own advertisement to every other server in the network, once per cooldown period. SmartBump also offers server ratings, reports, leaderboards, statistics and bump reminders.

## 3. Eligibility
- You must follow [Discord's Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines) at all times.
- You must meet the minimum age required to use Discord in your country.
- Your server must follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines to take part in the network.

## 4. How the Network Works
- **Two-way participation.** To bump, your server must have a working ad channel where SmartBump can post advertisements from other servers. If SmartBump cannot post in your ad channel, your advertisements are paused and you cannot bump until it is fixed with `/setup`.
- **Cooldown.** Each server can be bumped once every 2 hours. The cooldown may change at any time.
- **Your advertisement.** Your ad is made from your server's name, icon, banner, member count, creation date, and the description, tags and colour you set in `/setup`. It includes a permanent invite link to your server that SmartBump creates. You are responsible for everything in your advertisement.
- **Description rules.** Descriptions must be 20–500 characters and may not contain invite links or mentions.
- **Featured ads.** In addition to ads from bumps, SmartBump may post featured advertisements chosen by the SmartBump team to the network on a regular schedule. Featured ads are labelled "📌 Featured server".
- **Ads already posted.** Advertisements are normal Discord messages in other servers. Once posted, they stay in those servers' channels unless those servers delete them.

## 6. Ratings and Reports

- SmartBump does not check every advertisement before it is posted and is not responsible for the content of advertised servers. Join servers at your own risk.

A blacklisted server that adds SmartBump again will be left automatically.
